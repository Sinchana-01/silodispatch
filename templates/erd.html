Entity Relationship Diagram - SiloDispatch

Entity Explanations

  • Order: Represents customer orders including address, weight, mode of payment, and OTP status.
  • Driver: Delivery personnel who fulfill batches of orders.
  • Batch: A collection of up to 25 orders grouped together for optimized delivery.
  • Payment: Tracks whether payment was made via COD, UPI, or prepaid and the settlement status.
  • Breadcrumb: Stores the GPS trail for driver movement, used for tracking.
