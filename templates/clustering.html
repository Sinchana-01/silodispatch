Clustering Logic - Order Batching & Driver Assignment

1. Order Batching

  • Orders are grouped into batches of max 25kg or max 30 orders.
  • Clustering is based on:
    • Pincode — Simple & fast grouping of nearby addresses.
    • Geo-based clustering — Uses latitude/longitude with Haversine or K-Means.
  • Each batch is assigned a unique ID and stored in the batches table.

2. Driver Assignment

  • Drivers are matched to batches based on proximity to cluster centroid.
  • If GPS data is available, Haversine distance is calculated between driver and batch center.
  • Driver with lowest distance is auto-assigned (unless done manually).

3. Optimization

  • Improves delivery speed and reduces fuel usage.
  • Logic can be adapted to traffic or time windows in future versions.
